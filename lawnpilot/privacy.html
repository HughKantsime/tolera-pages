LawnPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 15, 2026

LawnPilot helps homeowners plan and track lawn care tasks. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

LawnPilot does not collect personal information, track users, show third-party ads, or use third-party analytics SDKs.

Local App Data

Yard setup, lawn zones, task plans, treatment journal entries, product notes, photos, settings, and privacy controls are stored on your device for the app experience.

Camera, Photos, and Barcode Scanning

If you choose to use camera, photo, or barcode features, the app uses that access only to support the feature you requested. LawnPilot does not sell or share those materials.

Weather and Product Information

LawnPilot may use weather and product information to help present practical lawn care planning context. Do not rely on the app as a substitute for product labels, local rules, or professional guidance.

iCloud and Device Services

If iCloud or Apple system services are enabled on your device, Apple may handle related device data under your Apple ID and Apple's privacy policy. LawnPilot does not operate a user account system.

Your Controls

You can review, export, or delete LawnPilot data from inside the app where those controls are provided. You can also remove local app data by deleting the app from your device.
