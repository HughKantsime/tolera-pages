Layerborn Support

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Layerborn is a 3D print-farm idle game for iPhone and iPad.

Contact

For support, email hughkantsime@gmail.com.

Common Questions

  • Purchases: Optional purchases are handled through Apple's StoreKit. Use Restore Purchases in the Shop to restore non-consumable content.
  • Consumables: Gem purchases add gems to your in-game balance and are not restorable after they are consumed.
  • Save data: Progress is stored locally. If signed into iCloud, Layerborn can sync save data through Apple's iCloud key-value storage.
  • Ads: Layerborn does not include third-party ads.
