Layerborn Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

Layerborn is an idle fabrication game for iPhone and iPad. This policy explains what the app does and does not collect.

Data Collection

Layerborn does not collect personal information, track users, show third-party ads, or use third-party analytics SDKs.

Local Save Data

Game progress, settings, premium entitlement state, and balance information are stored on your device so the game can resume where you left off.

iCloud Sync

If you are signed into iCloud, Layerborn may use Apple's iCloud key-value storage to sync your save between your own devices. This data is handled by Apple under your Apple ID and is not sent to a Layerborn server.

In-App Purchases

Layerborn uses Apple's StoreKit for optional in-app purchases. Apple processes purchase transactions. Layerborn stores only the purchase and entitlement state needed to grant gems or unlock non-consumable content in the game.

Children's Privacy

Layerborn is not designed to collect personal information from children. The app does not include social features, user-generated content, web browsing, or targeted advertising.
